# Remote control (phone)

Tick **Remote control (phone / claude.ai)** when
[starting a conversation](new-conversation.md) and you can continue it
from the **Claude mobile app** or **claude.ai** too — e.g. check on a
long task from your phone and answer Claude's question.

- The link appears in the conversation's header in
  [Conversations](conversations.md) and in its queue detail (`Enter` on
  the Dashboard). It also shows up in the Claude app's list of your
  sessions.
- Messages you send from the phone and from ccc go into the same
  conversation; ccc's [task queue](tasks.md) keeps working too — a task is
  pasted in whenever Claude is idle.
- Tool approvals for such a conversation are answered in the session
  itself — in its pane in ccc or on the phone.
- If Claude hits a usage limit mid-work, a continue message is queued
  first and sent after the reset (or when you press `r`, with
  auto-continue off). See [Usage limits](limits.md).
- Tokens of every reply — also ones to messages from the phone — are
  counted in [Analytics](analytics.md).
- [Archiving](archive.md) the conversation stops it; restoring starts it
  again.
